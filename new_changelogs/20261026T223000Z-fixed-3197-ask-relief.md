- **A core waiting in an ask keeps its other actors running.** An ask made
  in a handler blocks its core's scheduler thread until the reply, and
  nothing ran the core meanwhile: its queued messages stayed queued and its
  other actors waited. An actor on another core that, answering, asked one of
  them timed out, and so did the first ask. A millisecond into the wait, and
  every millisecond after (50 µs while there is work), the waiting thread now
  delivers what is queued for its actors and hands each one with work to a
  core that runs it: the core waiting on that very actor (whose wait steps
  it), else an idle one. An actor with nothing in flight is moved; one with
  messages still queued is lent, stepped there while it stays its core's, so
  no message of it is overtaken. Nothing runs inside the waiting handler, so
  an actor that asks the waiting one still waits for its handler to finish
  (#3197).
